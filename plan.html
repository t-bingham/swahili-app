🇹🇿 Swahili Learning App — Fresh Action Plan

Prioritised, build-ready engineering & content plan from a full multi-lens review.
Review date: 15 June 2026 · Lenses: beginner + intermediate learner simulation, Swahili linguistics audit, pedagogy/competitor research, code & accessibility audit · Rationale & sources for every item: research.html
Headline. The app's foundation is genuinely strong — FSRS scheduling, scaffolded exercises, a dedicated grammar track, an excellent onboarding flow, and hand-curated vocabulary that is natural and culturally grounded. The build is clean (0 TS errors). But one issue outranks everything: ~4,700 machine-generated grammar cards teach incorrect Swahili (verified directly against public/swahili_default.db). That must be fixed before the conjugation/grammar tracks are treated as teaching-ready. Everything else is upside.

Phase 0 · Content correctness — fix the curriculum (BLOCKER)

All 25,353 cards are generated. The hand-curated vocabulary core is excellent and needs nothing. The risk is entirely in template-generated grammar — which means a handful of generator fixes corrects thousands of cards at once. Counts below were confirmed by querying the seed DB directly.

✓ Implemented (15 Jun 2026). Corrections are regenerated from morphological components by scripts/fix-grammar-content.cjs (applied to public/swahili_default.db) and mirrored as a v2 migration src/database/grammarFixes.ts so existing users' cloned DBs are repaired on open. Build passes; verified directly against the seed DB and against a live migrated user DB (0 residual non-words).
P0.1✓ DoneBlockerEffort: Low–Med

Fix the three negative-tense conjugation rules & regenerate (~3,636 cards)

The negative-tense generator applies the wrong morphology for all 159 verbs:

  • Negative present must change final -a → -i: sisoma → sisomi
  • Negative past uses -ku-, not -li-: silisoma → sikusoma
  • Negative perfect uses -ja- ("not yet"), not -me-: simesoma → sijasoma

Fixed in: scripts/fix-grammar-content.cjs (rewrites public/swahili_default.db) + src/database/grammarFixes.ts (v2 migration for existing users)

Confidence: Certain. Was teaching non-existent/incorrect verb forms to every learner who reached negation.

P0.2✓ DoneBlockerEffort: Medium

Rewrite the adjective–noun concord generator & regenerate (~1,193 cards)

The adjective generator prepends a literal class letter instead of applying concord, producing non-words:

  • N-class nasal assimilation: nbaya → mbaya; nkubwa → kubwa (nbaya/nkubwa are not Swahili words)
  • Animate N-class (people) take M-Wa concord: daktari nzuri → daktari mzuri
  • Class-5 zero prefix: gari jizuri → gari zuri
  • U-class abstract nouns take m-/n- concord (Likely): upole uzuri → upole mzuri

Fixed in: scripts/fix-grammar-content.cjs + src/database/grammarFixes.ts (regenerates concord from noun_class + animacy + number, with overrides for mistagged nouns)

Confidence: Certain for N-class / animacy / class-5; Likely for U-class — confirm with native speaker.

P0.3✓ DoneHighEffort: Low

Fix monosyllabic-verb negatives, subjunctive & object-infix forms

  • Monosyllabic negatives compound the P0.1 bug: hakula → hali, halikula → hakula, hamekula → hajala
  • Subjunctive of ja: akuje → aje (ku- drops in subjunctive; reference-confirmed)
  • Object infix + monosyllabic stem: ananikula → ananila (Likely)

Source: same conjugation generator (monosyllabic-stem handling)

P0.4Partly doneLowEffort: Low

Minor content cleanups

  • ndio → standard ndiyo ✓ (done — seed + migration)
  • Habitual husoma is invariant — six per-person cards glossed "I/you/he [habitual]" mislead; collapse to one card. Deferred: structural change to unit composition; needs a curriculum decision (the Swahili form is already correct, so this is pedagogical polish, not a correctness bug).
  • De-duplicate cross-unit repeats. Deferred: these are the same word intentionally seeded into multiple units (distinct ids), not duplicate rows — blindly deleting would leave units short of cards. Needs a "already seen in unit X" cross-reference instead of a delete.
